The problem
Fintech onboarding was a mess — and everyone felt it

New joiners struggled with three things at once: complex financial processes, fast-moving team structures, and jargon-heavy docs that assume you already know the industry. If you're coming from a different market or a non-fintech background? Even harder.

No docs
No centralised place to read about how the team works, who owns what, or what the processes actually are.
Hard to read
Even when docs exist, they're full of fintech jargon that's impossible to follow if you're new to the industry.
Who to ask?
New hires spend weeks just figuring out who the right person is — instead of actually doing their job.
Our solution
Meet Onboarding Buddy — your AI teammate from Day 1

We fed all our team materials into an AI agent — processes, team structure, who's in charge of what — and wrapped it in a simple onboarding flow anyone can follow, no matter where they're coming from.

Step 01
Guided intro
Walk through the team, roles & how everything works — like a real orientation, but on-demand.
Step 02
Action checklist
Know exactly what to read and do in Week 1. No guessing, no anxiety.
Step 03
Ask anything
Buddy explains in plain language — no jargon assumed, no question too basic.
Step 04
Always there
24/7 support — no waiting for a reply, no bothering a senior at a bad time.
